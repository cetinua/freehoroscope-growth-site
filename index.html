02 / WHAT WE'VE DECIDED

Make the core stronger.
Build beyond daily.

These directions were agreed during Phase 05. They are not a production deployment approval.

Build direction agreed01

Weekly + monthly
for all 12 signs.

Two distinct forecast formats, developed using a shared platform. No sign-by-sign public rollout based on keyword difficulty.

Weekly 350–550 words*Monthly 700–1,100 words*
*Flexible editorial starting ranges, not Google ranking requirements.
Improve selectively02

Keep Today / Tomorrow.

Preserve the working page structure and URLs. Investigate more specific writing, less repetitive descriptions, and concise date-aware titles—without unnecessary lengthening.

Primary demonstrated traffic source
Avoid broad changes03

No mass rewrites.

Compatibility and generic profiles have limited observed US clicks. Retain for now; do not force indexation, delete pages at scale, or infer a sitewide penalty.

Revisit only with new evidence
The product principle: a weekly forecast should interpret a week, and a monthly forecast should interpret a month. Neither should be a stitched-together collection of daily forecasts. Every astronomical claim must be grounded in the available event data.
